All Types of Allergies

An allergy occurs when your body’s immune system sees a certain substance as harmful. It reacts by causing an allergic reaction. Substances that cause allergic reactions are allergens.

There are many types of allergies like:

Drug Allergy:True allergies to drugs (medicines) occur in only a small number of people. Most drug reactions are not allergic, but are side effects of the properties of the medicine.

Food Allergy:There are different types of allergic reactions to foods. There are differences between IgE-mediated allergies, non-IgE mediated allergies and food intolerances.

Insects Allergy:Bees, wasps, hornets, yellow jackets and fire ants are the most common stinging insects that cause an allergic reaction.Non-stinging insects can also cause allergic reactions. The most common are cockroaches and the insect-like dust mite.

Latex Allergy:A latex allergy is an allergic reaction to the proteins extracted from the rubber tree. Rubber latex gloves, balloons, condoms, and other products made from this source contain latex.

Lungs Diseases

Lung disease is any problem in the lungs that prevents the lungs from working properly. There are three main types of lung disease:

  1. Airway diseases:These diseases affect the tubes (airways) that carry oxygen and other gases into and out of the lungs. They usually cause a narrowing or blockage of the airways.
  2. Lung tissue diseases:These diseases affect the structure of the lung tissue. Scarring or inflammation of the tissue makes the lungs unable to expand fully (restrictive lung disease). This makes it hard for the lungs to take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide.
  3. Lung circulation diseases:These diseases affect the blood vessels in the lungs. They are caused by clotting, scarring, or inflammation of the blood vessels.

The most common lung diseases include:

  • Asthma
  • Collapse of part or all of the lung (pneumothorax or atelectasis)
  • Abnormal buildup of fluid in the lungs (pulmonary edema)
  • Blocked lung artery (pulmonary embolus)
  • Lung infection (pneumonia)
