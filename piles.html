Piles

Piles (haemorrhoids) are lumps inside and around your bottom (anus). They often get better on their own after a few days. There are things you can do to treat and prevent piles.

Symptoms

  • bright red blood after you poo
  • an itchy anus
  • lumps around your anus
  • pain around your anus

Stomach Diseases

A stomach upset can occur due to overeating or eating too quickly. If not addressed in time, it may lead to chronic conditions such as IBS, stomach ulcers, chronic gastritis, appendicitis, or Crohn’s disease.

Symptoms

  • Gas (flatulence or belching)
  • Stomach pain
  • Heartburn
  • Feeling of discomfort after eating
